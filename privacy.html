Privacy Policy

Last updated: 8 November 2026

In short: your Revit models and project files never leave your computer. ReviX sends a small amount of technical data so we can keep the add-in working, see which tools are used and answer your messages. Registration is optional.

This policy covers the ReviX add-in for Autodesk Revit and this website. ReviX is a free tool made by an independent developer. "We" and "us" mean the ReviX developer.

What ReviX sends, and why

WhenWhat is sentWhy
When a ReviX tool opensA random ID for this installation, the computer name and the ReviX versionTo check the add-in is allowed to run and to show messages from the developer, for example about an important update
When you use a toolThe installation ID, the computer name and the name of the tool (for example "Renumber Doors")To learn which tools are useful and where to spend development time
When something goes wrongThe installation ID, the computer name, the part of ReviX that failed and the error messageTo find and fix bugs
If you register (optional)The name, email and phone number you type, plus the installation ID, computer name and versionTo send you updates about ReviX. Registration is never required to use the tools, and you can skip it
If you send feedback or answer the surveyWhat you write, with the installation ID and the computer nameTo read your message and reply to you inside Revit

ReviX does not send your Revit models, drawings or element data to us. Error messages are sent as Windows reports them, so in rare cases one may contain a file path.

AI features

  • AI Connect adds ReviX to the settings of an AI app you already use (for example Claude, ChatGPT or Gemini) on your computer. The AI app then talks to Revit on your computer. What you ask the AI app, and the model information it reads to answer you, is handled by that AI provider under its own privacy policy. It does not pass through ReviX servers.
  • AI Chat uses an API key that you enter yourself. The key is stored on your computer, encrypted with Windows data protection for your Windows account. Your messages and the model information needed to answer them go directly from your computer to the provider you picked (Anthropic, OpenAI or Google), under that provider's terms. ReviX does not receive them.

Where the data is kept

The data in the table above is stored in a private Google Sheet owned by the ReviX developer and received through Google Apps Script, so Google processes it on our behalf. Only the developer can see it. We do not sell or rent it, and we do not share it with anyone except as needed to run this service or when the law requires it.

We keep it for as long as it helps us run and improve ReviX. You can ask us to delete your data at any time (see below).

Data on your computer

ReviX keeps small settings files in your Windows user folder, for example whether you registered, how many times you have used it and your AI Chat settings. Uninstalling ReviX removes the add-in. You can delete these files at any time.

This website

This site is hosted on GitHub Pages and uses Google Fonts. It has no analytics, no ads and no cookies of its own. GitHub and Google may log basic technical data such as your IP address when they serve the page. Downloads come from GitHub Releases.

Your choices and rights

  • You can skip registration. ReviX stops reminding you after a few skips.
  • You can ask what data we hold about your installation, or ask us to correct or delete it.
  • Uninstalling ReviX stops all data being sent.

Children

ReviX is a professional tool for architects and engineers and is not aimed at children.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date at the top.
